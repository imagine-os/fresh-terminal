FreshStack

The default stack under every Fresh Terminal stage. Each piece has one job and a documented exit, so the kit stays simple and no dependency is forever. Switch the mode to see how each piece works when you use our system, bring your own keys, or hold everything yourself.

live written, not wired planned

Ours: you use Fresh Terminal's accounts and pay pass-through cost from the ledger. Your keys: you paste your own keys in Settings and calls go straight to the vendor. Self-host: you run each piece yourself, separated from us.

The promise

  1. One folder, one instruction at the root, one key to start.
  2. Every dependency is listed here with its status and its exit. Nothing hidden.
  3. Three modes, same product: ours, your keys, self-host. Moving between them is settings, not a rewrite.
  4. Each piece is replaced the day we can do its job simpler ourselves. The list is meant to get shorter.
