## Shipped

- D1 migration `0002_credits.sql`: `anon_devices`, account grant columns, `spend_daily`.
- `router/src/credits.ts`: signed device ids, the meter on paid endpoints (grant, two soft prompts, then sign-in; account grant; daily anonymous cap; per-IP and per-network rate limits; 60 KB anonymous body cap; default models and no escalation signed out), `GET /credits`, `POST /credits/device`; `/health` reports `credits`.
