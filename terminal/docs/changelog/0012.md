- Koi: A photographic (Poly Haven CC0 panorama, pebble bed, scanned rocks, physical water) — kept. B painterly (Kuwahara filter over A) — blocky, rejected (`?look=painted`). C v2 re-framed — the same faults, rejected. Rounds on A: (1) panorama turned to face the pagoda and trees, koi inside out fixed, greener water, bushes that read as twigs dropped, foreground rock moved out of the sightline, 4096 px sky; (2) planar reflection of the bridge and banks, bed tiling broken up, more ferns, portrait layout and 4K chrome. Stopped when a third round showed no clear gain.

## Honest shortcomings

- One midday photo lights the pond, so dusk, night and dawn are colour grades. The koi are procedural (no reachable permissively licensed koi model), convincing from above, simpler from the side. Ripples are analytic rings. Headless fps is not a real number; a phone and TV check is plan task `koi-v3-device-check`.
- Canvas screenshots are made by a script, not live; new cards say "No screenshot yet" until it runs (plan task `canvas-thumbs-ci`). The live preview runs the real page, so the koi pond preview costs a WebGL context.
