# 0010 — readable text over photo and material skins

Date: 2026-09-29. Model: Opus 5.5. Canon C-079. Follows changelog 0005 (skins) and decision 0018.

## Why

Over the mossy stone photo, the transcript meta lines, the licence credits and the "Result:" line were barely readable: the theme's dim phosphor greens (`--fg-faint` #177a31) sat on a green photo. The 72% veil over the whole stage dulled the photo and still did not help small text.

## Shipped

- `shared/src/ui/readable.ts`: `readableOver(tones, theme)` finds the weakest scrim (theme background colour, 0–96% in 2% steps) that keeps text, muted text, faint text and the accent at WCAG AA (4.5:1) over the skin's darkest, lightest and average tones. It picks neutral ink (#f5f5f0 or #111318) and neutral muted and faint greys. An accent that shares the skin's hue, or that cannot reach AA even on the plain scrim, is replaced with ink, so small text is never tinted the skin colour. Helpers: `tonesFromPixels` (3rd and 97th luminance percentiles), `colorsInBackground` and `tonesFromColors` (for procedural materials), `sameHue`, `blend`, `contrastRgb`.
- `app/src/skins/tones.ts`: samples each skin image, using its thumbnail when there is one, on a 48×48 canvas. The result is cached per URL. An image that cannot be read (no CORS, still loading) gets the worst case (black to white), so the scrim is never too weak.
- Shell: every stage or bar skin with an image or a material background gets `data-readable` and scoped `--fg`, `--fg-muted`, `--fg-faint`, `--accent`, `--scrim`, `--scrim-a` and `--scrim-blur`. Tones are composited under the region's veil first, because that is what text sits on. Image skins now use a 25% veil, down from 72%, so the photo stays vivid.
- CSS: on the stage, the transcript sits on the scrim with an 8px backdrop blur. The stage padding keeps the photo visible around them. Bars (top bar, sidebar, composer) get the scrim as an inset layer that leaves a 4px frame of the image.
- Tests: `shared/src/ui/readable.test.ts` checks AA for every text colour over moss, the worst case, no scrim when a material is already dark, dark ink on light themes, stop and pixel parsing, and hue matching. `app/src/__tests__/skins.test.tsx` checks the Shell layer end to end.

## Not wired yet

- Sampling images from hosts without CORS: they get the worst-case scrim (62% on a dark theme, against 44% for the sampled moss), which is readable but hides more of the photo. A router proxy for the thumbnail would fix it.
