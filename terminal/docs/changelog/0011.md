# 0011 — daily caps for signed-in free usage; the counter matches /credits

Date: 2026-09-29. Model: Opus 5.5. Coordinator follow-up to Canon C-074 ("add a daily cap for signed-in accounts before Clerk production"). Decision 0021 (appended). Canon C-080.

## Shipped

- Router (`router/src/credits.ts`): signed-in free usage now stops for the day at **$1 per account** (price, Worker var `ACCOUNT_DAILY_MICRO`) or **$10 across all signed-in accounts** (provider cost, `ACCOUNT_DAILY_TOTAL_COST_MICRO`), whichever comes first. The answer is `402 account_daily_cap` with "Daily free usage reached. It resets at 00:00 UTC, or use your key." Days are UTC; per-account spend is kept in `spend_daily` under scope `acct:<account id>`, next to the existing `account` and `anon` totals. No migration needed.
- `GET /credits` sets `daily_cap_reached` for accounts too; `/health` reports both numbers. "Your key" (bring your own) goes from the browser to OpenRouter and is never capped.
- App: the same line in the transcript for `account_daily_cap` (EN/ES).
- **Bug fixed (Justin's screenshot): the top-bar "$ used" said $0.0000 while `/credits` said $0.23 of $0.25 left.** The chip tagger calls `/tag` on every typing pause; the meter charged them, but they never reached the ledger. `/tag` now returns a charge entry (`what: chips.tag`, price = cost, the tagger tier has no margin) and the app appends it, as `/route` and `/skin/*` already did. The meter charges from that same entry, so the counter and `GET /credits` move together. The anonymous device id is also kept in memory when localStorage is blocked, so a private window does not ask for a new device on every call.
- Voice sessions (`/realtime/session`) still put an *estimate* on the ledger and charge nothing against free usage; they are the one place the two can differ (not wired yet: realtime usage pricing).
- Copy: "stages" instead of "boxes" in the sign-in tip, the sync line and the router's sync message (EN) and "escenarios" (ES). Internal ids, D1 tables and `/sync/boxes` are unchanged.
- Tests: one tag call and one route call through the real router and meter, then counter delta = credits delta (154 micro-dollars); one account capped while another is not; the all-accounts cap stops every account while signed-out devices keep their own cap; defaults $1 and $10.

- `sync-smoke` also makes one `/tag` call and checks that the credits delta equals the entry price.
