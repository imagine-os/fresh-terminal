## Reply summary

Koi pond v2 is a tiltable three.js scene: an arched bridge, an analytic sky with clouds, lily pads of any shape (circles and rounded rectangles that carry cards), stone paths, 12 koi with swimming waves, area-ratio caustics and a height-field ripple simulation. Tilt with a drag, two fingers, the arrow keys or the slider; light presets dawn, day, dusk and night; quality auto/high/low; calm mode; a dev panel edits pads and paths as JSON through `window.pond`. It replaces v1 at `pages/koi.html`; v1 stays at `pages/koi-v1.html`. Third-party notices: `pages/koi-LICENSES.md` (three.js MIT, koi-pond-garden MIT). What still looks fake is listed in changelog 0004. QA screenshots: `docs/qa/koi2-*.png`.
