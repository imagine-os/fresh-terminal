20. **Is 10% enough to cover the fees?** (2026-09-29, C-103, C-105) On a $10 Clerk refill: Stripe 2.9% + $0.30 = $0.59 and Clerk Billing 0.7% = $0.07, so $0.66; the $10 buys $9.09 of model use at +10%, so the markup is $0.91 and we keep about $0.25 before hosting. OpenRouter adds a fee when we buy its credit by card (5.5% when last checked; not re-verified today); on $9.09 that is about $0.50, which leaves a $10 refill about $0.25 short. Break-even with all three is about 13%, larger refills, OpenRouter credit bought another way, or people choosing a higher markup (C-105). Referral shares (C-107) take half the markup on referred friends' paid usage for a year. Open: keep 10% as the default, raise the default or the 5% floor, or set a minimum refill.
