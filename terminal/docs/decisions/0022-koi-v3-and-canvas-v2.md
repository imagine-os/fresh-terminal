# 0022 — koi pond v3 and canvas v2

Date: 2026-09-29. Model: Opus 5.5 (all of it). Prompt 0018 (Canon prompts 60, 61). Canon: C-082 (koi), C-083 (canvas). Replaces the look, not the data, of decisions 0011 (canvas) and the koi v2 build in changelog 0004.

## Koi pond v3

- **What was wrong with v2** (screenshots at 390 and 1280, `docs/qa/koi3-before-*`): it opened at a 65° tilt, neither a table top nor a view; the water was flat and milky with no depth, the bed a bright even Voronoi mosaic; lilac, texture-less rocks; lime procedural grass; small koi pasted on top of the water; a pink, spindly bridge; a flat grass plane running to an empty pastel horizon, so "looking into the distance" showed nothing; the content card floated in mid-air in the tilted view; six pills and a slider cluttered the top.
- **Opening shot:** straight down (camera elevation 90°), the pond filling the view: about 6.6 m of water top to bottom on landscape, 4.3 m across on portrait. Three content cards (Today, Pinned, Inbox) lie on giant Victoria-style lily pads and are sized so they render at about 1:1 CSS pixels in this view, so their text is crisp and readable as a desk.
- **Tilt:** ~1.5 s after the first frame the camera tilts up (eased, ~2.4 s) to elevation 9.5° across the pond: bridge in the middle distance, far bank, the garden and sky. The first drag, scroll, arrow key or button press takes over from the automatic tilt. With `prefers-reduced-motion` there is no automatic tilt and the switch jumps. Controls: the Top-down / Forward switch (top left, `T`), vertical drag, wheel or trackpad scroll, ↑/↓ for tilt and ←/→ to turn, the slider on wide screens. Cards hinge up to 16° (6° on portrait) as you tilt so they stay attached to the pads and do not block the view.
- **Look (chosen by best-of-3):** A photographic garden (chosen) — CC0 Poly Haven panorama "Chinese Garden" as sky, background and image-based light, with the sun direction taken from the photo; river-pebble bed with a macro variation layer; underwater light absorption by depth and view path (red goes first, water turns green-dark with depth); animated two-layer caustics that stop in shadows; soft sun shadows from pads, koi and bridge onto the bed; water as a transmissive, refracting surface with Fresnel reflection of the panorama plus a planar reflection of the bridge, banks and rocks; 13 koi in ten varieties with painted skins, translucent fins and a swimming wave; mossy scanned rocks, ferns and shrubs; a vermilion taiko bridge with hinoki deck and bronze caps. B painterly (a Kuwahara filter over A): blocky and muddy, rejected (still at `?look=painted`). C v2 re-framed (v2 at 88° and 14°): the problems above remain, rejected. Two improvement rounds on A followed (inside-out koi fixed, greener water, 4096-wide panorama, bushes that read as twigs removed, rim rocks cleared from the forward sightline, bed tiling broken up).
- **Weight:** `koi.js` 632 KB minified (three.js is most of it, ~165 KB gzipped); assets 2.4 MB on high, 1.4 MB on low. Low tier (auto on touch or small screens, or `?q=low`): no refraction pass, no planar reflection, 2048 panorama, fewer fish and plants, lighter terrain (about 96 k triangles drawn per frame against about 850 k on high). Headless Chromium uses a software GPU, so it cannot measure real frame rates; the on-page fps counter is the number to watch on a phone.
- **API kept:** `window.pond` (`addPad`, `removePad`, `updatePad`, `addPath`, `removePath`, `setCamera({tilt 8–90, azimuth})`, `getCamera()` now also returns `view` and `t`, new `setView('top-down'|'forward')`, `setTimeOfDay`, `list`, `applyJSON`) and `window.koi`. URL: `?view=forward`, `?tilt=<deg>`, `?q=low|high`, `?tod=`, `?look=painted`. Page actions are declared in the page (`#page-actions`).
- **Honest limits:** the panorama is one midday photo, so dawn, dusk and night are colour grades, not new light; the koi are procedural (no permissively licensed rigged koi model was found that the network here could reach); water ripples are analytic rings, not a simulation; the far bank is a photo, so moving the camera far from the centre would show parallax errors (the rig does not allow it).

## Canvas v2

- **What was wrong with v1** (`docs/qa/canvas2-before-*`): cards were empty green-on-black terminal boxes that showed raw hrefs and "1 mm · Enter opens", no pictures, no grouping, a fixed 3×3 grid; at 3840 the chrome was tiny while cards were huge, at 390 the cards were unreadable and the toolbar took three rows; a title repeated the breadcrumb.
- **Now:** a desk-coloured surface with a dot grid that moves with pan and zoom; square paper sheets with a hard edge and soft layered shadow scaled by thickness (1 mm paper, 10 mm images); a real screenshot on every sheet (`pnpm canvas:thumbs` writes `app/public/canvas/thumbs/<id>.jpg`); titles that never shrink below readable (counter-scaled with zoom); sections Terminal, Worlds, Docs and Archive as hand-drawn frames with handwritten labels (Caveat, OFL, self-hosted); the arrangement picked to fit the surface best (pure `bestLayout()` in `shared/src/canvas/layout.ts`), one column on phones; minimap with click and drag to jump; Fit (0), 100% (1), zoom (+/−, pinch, Ctrl/⌘+wheel), pan (drag, two-finger scroll, arrows); Tab through cards, Enter selects and shows a live preview (sandboxed iframe of the real page), Enter again opens, Alt+arrows or mouse drag move a card (kept per browser, "Tidy up" puts them back). The toolbar has no title (the breadcrumb already says it). Light and dark follow the chosen theme's scheme.
- **Look (chosen by best-of-3):** B sketch hybrid (chosen): hand-drawn frames and labels for charm, clean sans titles and square sheets for polish. A plain paper (clean but anonymous, `?look=paper`), C drafting table (blue grid, busy, `?look=drafting`), rejected.
- **Data:** cards gain optional `section`, `thumb` and `archived` (zod). Saved cards from older visits get these from the seed by id. Positions in `cards.json` are only used by the archived v1. New seed cards: Landing, Koi pond v2, Koi pond v1, Canvas v1 (the last three archived).
- **Honest limits:** screenshots are made by a script, not live; a card added from the toolbar shows "No screenshot yet" (not wired until the script runs). The live preview loads the whole page (the koi pond runs WebGL inside it).
